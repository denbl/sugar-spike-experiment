    Design·Impact model

    Experiment design

    A push notification to walk when glucose starts rising after a meal puts more weight-loss users on-track, and on-track users stay to month 3 more often.

    • Big post-meal glucose dips: ~300 kcal more eaten a day. ZOE PREDICT, Wyatt 2021
    • 2–10 min of light walking after a meal: ~17% lower post-meal glucose. Buffey 2022, meta-analysis
    • On-track users retain better: 74% vs 41%. National DPP, n = 41,203
    • Assumption A lower spike means less hunger for people without diabetes. This test checks it.
    Who
    New weight-loss users, first 14 days.
    Split
    50/50. Control gets no push notification, but the trigger is logged.
    Trigger
    + mg/dL within min after a meal · · max a day.

    Decision
